# Operators in C#

## Arithmetic Operators
- `+`: Addition
- `-`: Subtraction
- `*`: Multiplication
- `/`: Division
- `%`: Modulus (remainder)
- `++`: Increment
- `--`: Decrement

## Assignment Operators
- `=`: Assign
- `+=`: Add and assign
- `-=`: Subtract and assign

## Comparison Operators
- `==`: Equal to
- `!=`: Not equal
- `>`: Greater than
- `<`: Less than
- `>=`: Greater than or equal
- `<=`: Less than or equal

## Logical Operators
- `&&`: Logical AND
- `||`: Logical OR
- `!`: Logical NOT
